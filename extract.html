The four asks

What the new buyers ask on the first call, and the change that answers it.

1 · Coverage

Coverage is a denominator, not a success rate

"You crawl 3,000 counties. Which ones were missing last month, and is the gap random?"

Ship the roster and the exclusion list with every delivery, so buyers see the shape of the gap, not just the rate.

2 · Freshness

Freshness is a distribution, not a schedule

"Your SLA says monthly. What's the p99 age of the rows in the file you just sent me?"

Report age per source, at the tail, from when each value was last confirmed, not last attempted.

3 · Provenance

Provenance has to survive the delivery

"This says 400 employees. Show me the page that said so, as it looked when you read it."

Link every shipped field to its snapshot and parser version, and treat raw-page retention as a contract term.

4 · Reproducibility

Reproducibility is the one nobody prices in

"If my benchmark score moves next quarter, was that my model or your data?"

Treat a parser change like a schema migration: diff it against stored pages, then tell buyers what moved.
